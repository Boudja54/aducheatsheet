ADU Regulations in Everett, Washington

Snohomish County's largest city, Everett permits ADUs by right in all residential zones under Washington's HB 1337. The city prohibits short-term rentals of ADUs, ensuring they serve the local long-term housing market.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Everett as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

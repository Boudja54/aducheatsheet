Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Corpus Christi

Can I build an ADU in Corpus Christi?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Corpus Christi?

ADUs in Corpus Christi can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Corpus Christi planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Corpus Christi?

ADUs in Corpus Christi generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. Texas has no statewide ADU mandate — the exact setback figures come from Corpus Christi's own zoning code, so confirm them with the city planning department before you design.

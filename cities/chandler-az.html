Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Chandler

Can I build an ADU in Chandler?

This Arizona city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Confirm your zone's eligibility with the planning department first.

How big can my ADU be in Chandler?

Under Chandler zoning rules, a detached ADU can reach 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Chandler?

Expect plan review in Chandler to take 4 to 8 weeks for a complete application. Simple attached conversions usually move faster than new detached construction. Backlog in Maricopa County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

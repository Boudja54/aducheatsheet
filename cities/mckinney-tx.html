Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in McKinney

Can I build an ADU in McKinney?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

How big can my ADU be in McKinney?

In McKinney, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in McKinney?

Expect plan review in McKinney to take 6 to 12 weeks for a complete application. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Collin County processing times vary seasonally, so the range above is a realistic guide.

ADU Regulations in Pueblo, Colorado

Located in Pueblo County along the Arkansas River, the city's ADU program operates entirely under local zoning authority. Colorado has no statewide ADU law, meaning Pueblo's municipal code is the definitive source for all ADU requirements.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Pueblo as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Pueblo

Can I build an ADU in Pueblo?

Pueblo allows ADUs under Colorado state law (HB 24-1152). The owner occupancy requirement applies.

How big can my ADU be in Pueblo?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Pueblo is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Pueblo?

Plan review in Pueblo typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

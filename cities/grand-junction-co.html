ADU Regulations in Grand Junction, Colorado

In Mesa County on Colorado's Western Slope, Grand Junction allows ADUs in lower-density single-family zones. Without a Colorado state ADU mandate, the city's 25% lot coverage limit and owner occupancy requirement are locally determined.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Grand Junction as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Grand Junction

Can I build an ADU in Grand Junction?

Grand Junction allows ADUs in single-family zones. Owner occupancy and off-street parking must be provided.

How big can my ADU be in Grand Junction?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Grand Junction is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Grand Junction?

Plan review in Grand Junction typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

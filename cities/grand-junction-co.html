Can I build an ADU in Grand Junction?

How big can my ADU be in Grand Junction?

The maximum size for an ADU in Grand Junction is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Grand Junction Building & Safety Department.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Grand Junction

Can I build an ADU in Grand Junction?

Grand Junction allows ADUs in single-family zones. Owner occupancy and off-street parking must be provided.

How big can my ADU be in Grand Junction?

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Grand Junction? ADUs in Grand Junction can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Grand Junction planning department before designing your project.

What about setbacks and other requirements in Grand Junction?

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Grand Junction? ADUs in Grand Junction generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

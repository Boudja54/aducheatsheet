ADU Regulations in Oceanside, California

Updated September 29, 2026

Along the San Diego County coastline, Oceanside's ADU regulations include additional review layers for properties within the Coastal Zone. The California Coastal Commission may require a coastal development permit alongside the standard ADU building permit.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Oceanside as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Oceanside

Can I build an ADU in Oceanside?

Oceanside allows ADUs per state law. If your property is in the coastal zone, additional Coastal Commission review may be required.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Oceanside?

ADUs in Oceanside can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Oceanside planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Oceanside?

ADUs in Oceanside generally require setbacks of 4 ft rear, 4 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

ADU Regulations in Lakewood, Washington

In Pierce County south of Tacoma, Lakewood follows Washington's HB 1337 statewide ADU rules. The city's location near Joint Base Lewis-McChord creates strong rental demand for accessory units.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Lakewood as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Lakewood

Can I build an ADU in Lakewood?

This Washington city follows HB 1337 which limits local ADU restrictions. Parking may be waived near transit.

How big can my ADU be in Lakewood?

In Lakewood, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Lakewood?

Most ADU applications in Lakewood clear plan review in 6 to 10 weeks. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Backlog in Pierce County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

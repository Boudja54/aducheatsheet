ADU Regulations in Aurora, Colorado

Colorado's third-largest city, Aurora permits ADUs through its local zoning code. Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate, so Aurora's municipal code establishes all requirements including minimum lot sizes and design review standards.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Aurora as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Aurora

Can I build an ADU in Aurora?

Aurora allows ADUs in single-family zones. The city has specific design standards and a minimum lot size requirement.

How big can my ADU be in Aurora?

Under Aurora zoning rules, a detached ADU can reach 1,200 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Aurora?

Plan review in Aurora typically runs 4 to 8 weeks, depending on how complete the application is. Detached builds take longer than attached conversions in most cases. Adams County / Arapahoe County processing times vary seasonally, so the range above is a realistic guide.

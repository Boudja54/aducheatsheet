Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Eugene

Can I build an ADU in Eugene?

Eugene allows ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. No owner occupancy requirement and parking is generally not required.

How big can my ADU be in Eugene?

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Eugene? ADUs in Eugene can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Eugene planning department before designing your project.

What about setbacks and other requirements in Eugene?

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Eugene? ADUs in Eugene generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

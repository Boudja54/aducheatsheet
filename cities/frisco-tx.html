Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Frisco

Can I build an ADU in Frisco?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

How big can my ADU be in Frisco?

In Frisco, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Frisco?

Plan review in Frisco typically runs 6 to 12 weeks, depending on how complete the application is. Detached builds take longer than attached conversions in most cases. Backlog in Collin County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

ADU Regulations in Hillsboro, Oregon

Washington County's largest city and home to Silicon Forest tech companies, Hillsboro permits ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. The growing demand for workforce housing near major employers like Intel drives interest in ADU construction.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Hillsboro as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Hillsboro

Can I build an ADU in Hillsboro?

Hillsboro follows Oregon's HB 2001. ADUs are permitted by right with no additional local restrictions for most properties.

How big can my ADU be in Hillsboro?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Hillsboro is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Hillsboro?

Plan review in Hillsboro typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

ADU Regulations in Santa Rosa, California

Nestled in Sonoma County, Santa Rosa has been proactive about ADUs since the 2017 wildfires, streamlining permit processes as part of the city's recovery and housing resilience strategy. The Urban Growth Boundary may influence lot coverage for larger properties.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Santa Rosa as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Santa Rosa

Can I build an ADU in Santa Rosa?

Santa Rosa has been proactive about ADUs since the 2017 wildfires, streamlining permit processes to encourage more housing.

How big can my ADU be in Santa Rosa?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Santa Rosa is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Santa Rosa?

Plan review in Santa Rosa typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Santa Rosa

Can I build an ADU in Santa Rosa?

Yes, Santa Rosa allows ADUs under California state law and local zoning ordinances. [ADDITIONAL_FAQ_1]

How big can my ADU be in Santa Rosa?

The maximum size for an ADU in Santa Rosa is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Do I need a permit for an ADU in Santa Rosa?

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Santa Rosa Building & Safety Department.

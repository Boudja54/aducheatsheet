Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Vallejo

Can I build an ADU in Vallejo?

Yes, Vallejo allows ADUs under California state law and local zoning ordinances. [ADDITIONAL_FAQ_1]

How big can my ADU be in Vallejo?

The maximum size for an ADU in Vallejo is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Vallejo Building & Safety Department.

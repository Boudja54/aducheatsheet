ADU Regulations in Vallejo, California

Situated in Solano County in the North Bay, Vallejo benefits from California's streamlined ADU laws while maintaining local design review standards. The city views ADUs as key to meeting its Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA) targets.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Vallejo as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Vallejo

Can I build an ADU in Vallejo?

Vallejo follows California state ADU law. The city encourages ADU construction as part of its housing element plan.

How big can my ADU be in Vallejo?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Vallejo is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Vallejo?

Plan review in Vallejo typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

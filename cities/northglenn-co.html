Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Northglenn

Can I build an ADU in Northglenn?

This Colorado city regulates ADUs through local ordinance. Confirm the minimum lot size and owner occupancy rules with the planning department.

How big can my ADU be in Northglenn?

Northglenn allows detached ADUs up to 1,000 square feet on qualifying lots. JADUs, where allowed locally, are limited to 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Northglenn?

Most ADU applications in Northglenn clear plan review in 4 to 8 weeks. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Adams County processing times vary seasonally, so the range above is a realistic guide.

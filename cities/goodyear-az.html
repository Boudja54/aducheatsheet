Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Goodyear

Can I build an ADU in Goodyear?

This Arizona city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Confirm your zone's eligibility with the planning department first.

How big can my ADU be in Goodyear?

Goodyear allows detached ADUs up to 1,000 square feet on qualifying lots. JADUs, where allowed locally, are limited to 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Goodyear?

Most ADU applications in Goodyear clear plan review in 4 to 8 weeks. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Maricopa County processing times vary seasonally, so the range above is a realistic guide.

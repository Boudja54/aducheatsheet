ADU Regulations in Federal Way, Washington

Situated in King County between Seattle and Tacoma, Federal Way permits ADUs under Washington's HB 1337 framework. The city's location along the I-5 corridor makes it an attractive market for ADU development.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Federal Way as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

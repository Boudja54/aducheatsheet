ADU Regulations in Fresno, California

As the heart of California's Central Valley, Fresno follows the statewide ADU framework established by AB 68 and AB 881. Fresno's Planning Department reviews ADU applications with attention to the city's specific zoning district requirements, particularly in neighborhoods zoned for single-family residential use.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Fresno as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Fresno

Can I build an ADU in Fresno?

Fresno permits ADUs in all residential zones following California state law. Check with the Fresno Planning Department for specific lot coverage limits.

How big can my ADU be in Fresno?

Under Fresno zoning rules, a detached ADU can reach 1,200 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Fresno?

Most ADU applications in Fresno clear plan review in 8 to 12 weeks. Detached builds take longer than attached conversions in most cases. Fresno County staff workload can push approvals toward the upper end of that range during busy months.

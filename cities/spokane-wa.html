ADU Regulations in Spokane, Washington

Eastern Washington's largest city, Spokane permits ADUs under Washington's HB 1337 which limits local restrictions. Spokane's municipal code requires owner occupancy and prohibits short-term rental use of ADUs, preserving them for long-term housing.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Spokane as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Spokane

Can I build an ADU in Spokane?

Spokane allows ADUs in single-family and multi-family zones. Owner occupancy is required and the ADU cannot be used as a short-term rental.

How big can my ADU be in Spokane?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Spokane is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Spokane?

Plan review in Spokane typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

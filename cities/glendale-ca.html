Updated September 29, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Glendale

Can I build an ADU in Glendale?

Glendale follows California state ADU law. Standard state minimums apply with no additional local restrictions for most properties.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Glendale?

ADUs in Glendale can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Glendale planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Glendale?

ADUs in Glendale generally require setbacks of 4 ft rear, 4 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

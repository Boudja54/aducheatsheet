Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Kirkland

Can I build an ADU in Kirkland?

Kirkland allows ADUs under Washington state law. Waterfront properties may need additional design review.

How big can my ADU be in Kirkland?

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Kirkland? ADUs in Kirkland can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Kirkland planning department before designing your project.

What about setbacks and other requirements in Kirkland?

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Kirkland? ADUs in Kirkland generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

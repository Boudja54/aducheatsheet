ADU Regulations in Beaverton, Oregon

Updated September 29, 2026

In Washington County west of Portland, Beaverton permits ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. The city's location along the MAX Light Rail line provides transit-oriented development opportunities with potential SDC reductions.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Beaverton as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Beaverton

Can I build an ADU in Beaverton?

Beaverton allows ADUs under Oregon state law. The city provides a streamlined process for standard ADU designs.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Beaverton?

ADUs in Beaverton can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Beaverton planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Beaverton?

ADUs in Beaverton generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) requires cities to allow ADUs in residential zones, but the exact setback figures come from Beaverton's own code — confirm them with the city before you design.

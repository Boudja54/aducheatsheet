ADU Regulations in Sammamish, Washington

In eastern King County, Sammamish allows ADUs under Washington's HB 1337 framework. The city's affluent lakeside neighborhoods apply design review standards to new accessory units.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Sammamish as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Sammamish

Can I build an ADU in Sammamish?

This Washington city follows HB 1337 which limits local ADU restrictions. Parking may be waived near transit.

How big can my ADU be in Sammamish?

In Sammamish, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Sammamish?

Expect plan review in Sammamish to take 6 to 10 weeks for a complete application. Detached builds take longer than attached conversions in most cases. Backlog in King County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

At the heart of the South Plains in Lubbock County, the city manages ADU approvals under local zoning authority. With no Texas statewide mandate, Lubbock's code sets all lot, setback, and design requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Lubbock

Can I build an ADU in Lubbock?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

How big can my ADU be in Lubbock?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Lubbock is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Lubbock?

Plan review in Lubbock typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

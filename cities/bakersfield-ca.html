Bakersfield ADU Regulations at a Glance

Here are the key zoning rules for Accessory Dwelling Units in Bakersfield, California. These regulations apply as of 2026.

Regulation Requirement
Maximum Size 1,200 sq ft
Property Setbacks 4 ft rear, 4 ft side
Extra Parking Required Required (1 space, tandem OK; waived near transit)
Owner Occupancy Not required (for ADUs; required for JADUs)

Additional Local Requirements

  • ADU must be at least 150 sq ft
  • Detached ADUs allowed up to 1,200 sq ft
  • Owner occupancy NOT required for ADUs (required for JADUs)
  • Parking: 1 space required (tandem OK) — waived for JADU, garage conversion, or within ½ mile transit

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Bakersfield

Can I build an ADU in Bakersfield?

Yes, Bakersfield allows ADUs under California state law and local zoning ordinances. [ADDITIONAL_FAQ_1]

How big can my ADU be in Bakersfield?

The maximum size for an ADU in Bakersfield is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Do I need a permit for an ADU in Bakersfield?

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Bakersfield Building & Safety Department.

Fort Collins ADU Regulations at a Glance

Here are the key zoning rules for Accessory Dwelling Units in Fort Collins, Colorado. These regulations apply as of 2026.

Regulation Requirement
Maximum Size 1,200 sq ft
Property Setbacks 5 ft rear, 5 ft side
Extra Parking Required Required (1 space)
Owner Occupancy Required

Additional Local Requirements

  • ADU on lots 3,500 sq ft minimum
  • Minimum ADU size: 300 sq ft
  • One ADU per single-family lot
  • Short-term rental of ADU prohibited

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Fort Collins

Can I build an ADU in Fort Collins?

Yes, Fort Collins allows ADUs under Colorado state law and local zoning ordinances. [ADDITIONAL_FAQ_1]

How big can my ADU be in Fort Collins?

The maximum size for an ADU in Fort Collins is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Do I need a permit for an ADU in Fort Collins?

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Fort Collins Building & Safety Department.

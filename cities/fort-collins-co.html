ADU Regulations in Fort Collins, Colorado

At the base of the Rocky Mountains in Larimer County, Fort Collins has one of Colorado's most developed ADU programs. The city allows ADUs on lots as small as 3,500 square feet with specific design standards reflecting the community's character.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Fort Collins as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

  • Minimum ADU size: 300 sq ft

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Fort Collins

Can I build an ADU in Fort Collins?

Fort Collins permits ADUs in lower-density neighborhoods. Owner occupancy is required and the ADU cannot be used as a vacation rental.

How big can my ADU be in Fort Collins?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Fort Collins is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Fort Collins?

Plan review in Fort Collins typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

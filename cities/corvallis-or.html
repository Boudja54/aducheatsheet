ADU Regulations in Corvallis, Oregon

Updated September 29, 2026

Home to Oregon State University in Benton County, Corvallis permits ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. Student housing demand makes accessory units a practical option for local property owners.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Corvallis as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Corvallis

Can I build an ADU in Corvallis?

This Oregon city allows ADUs by right under HB 2001. No owner occupancy requirement and minimal local restrictions.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Corvallis?

ADUs in Corvallis can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Corvallis planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Corvallis?

ADUs in Corvallis generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) requires cities to allow ADUs in residential zones, but the exact setback figures come from Corvallis's own code — confirm them with the city before you design.

ADU Regulations in Medford, Oregon

In southern Oregon's Jackson County, Medford allows ADUs by right under HB 2001. The city's affordable housing initiatives actively encourage accessory dwelling unit construction.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Medford as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Regulation Requirement
Maximum Size 1,200 sq ft
Property Setbacks 5 ft rear, 5 ft side

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Medford

Can I build an ADU in Medford?

This Oregon city allows ADUs by right under HB 2001. No owner occupancy requirement and minimal local restrictions.

How big can my ADU be in Medford?

Medford allows detached ADUs up to 1,200 square feet on qualifying lots. JADUs, where allowed locally, are limited to 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Medford?

Most ADU applications in Medford clear plan review in 5 to 9 weeks. Simple attached conversions usually move faster than new detached construction. Backlog in Jackson County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

ADU Regulations in Gresham, Oregon

Multnomah County's second-largest city, Gresham permits ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. The city actively encourages ADU construction through reduced System Development Charges (SDCs), making it an affordable option for property owners.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Gresham as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Gresham

Can I build an ADU in Gresham?

Gresham permits ADUs under Oregon law. The city offers SDC reductions for ADUs to encourage construction.

How big can my ADU be in Gresham?

Under Gresham zoning rules, a detached ADU can reach 1,200 square feet. When the property qualifies, a JADU conversion is capped at 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Gresham?

Plan review in Gresham typically runs 5 to 9 weeks, depending on how complete the application is. Detached builds take longer than attached conversions in most cases. Backlog in Multnomah County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

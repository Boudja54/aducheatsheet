Updated September 29, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Westminster

Can I build an ADU in Westminster?

Westminster allows ADUs in certain residential zones. Contact the planning department to confirm your zone eligibility.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Westminster?

ADUs in Westminster can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Westminster planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Westminster?

ADUs in Westminster generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate, so the exact setback figures come from Westminster's own zoning code — confirm them with the city planning department before you design.

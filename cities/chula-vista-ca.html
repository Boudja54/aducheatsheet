Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Chula Vista

Can I build an ADU in Chula Vista?

This California city follows the statewide AB 68 framework. Most single-family lots qualify for by-right ADU development.

How big can my ADU be in Chula Vista?

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Chula Vista? ADUs in Chula Vista can be up to 1,200 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Chula Vista planning department before designing your project.

What about setbacks and other requirements in Chula Vista?

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Chula Vista? ADUs in Chula Vista generally require setbacks of 4 ft rear, 4 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Boulder

Can I build an ADU in Boulder?

Yes, Boulder allows ADUs under Colorado state law and local zoning ordinances. [ADDITIONAL_FAQ_1]

How big can my ADU be in Boulder?

The maximum size for an ADU in Boulder is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback and lot coverage requirements.

Do I need a permit for an ADU in Boulder?

Yes, all ADUs require a building permit from the Boulder Building & Safety Department.

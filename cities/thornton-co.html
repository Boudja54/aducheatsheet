ADU Regulations in Thornton, Colorado

Adams County's largest city, Thornton regulates ADUs through its municipal code. Without statewide ADU legislation, the city imposes a 6,000 sq ft minimum lot size and requires property owners to occupy the residence.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Thornton as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

Can I build an ADU in Thornton?

How big can my ADU be in Thornton?

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Thornton? ADUs in Thornton can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Thornton planning department before designing your project.

What about setbacks and other requirements in Thornton?

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Thornton? ADUs in Thornton generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. California state law (AB 68) limits local setback requirements to 4 feet for existing structures, but always verify with local zoning.

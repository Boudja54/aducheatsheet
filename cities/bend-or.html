ADU Regulations in Bend, Oregon

In Deschutes County at the foot of the Cascades, Bend permits ADUs by right under Oregon's HB 2001. The city's booming housing market and tourism economy make accessory units a strong investment.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Bend as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Oregon's HB 2001 (2019) which requires ADU approval by-right in all residential zones statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Bend

Can I build an ADU in Bend?

This Oregon city allows ADUs by right under HB 2001. No owner occupancy requirement and minimal local restrictions.

How big can my ADU be in Bend?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Bend is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Bend?

Plan review in Bend typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

ADU Regulations in Rancho Cucamonga, California

In San Bernardino County, Rancho Cucamonga follows California's statewide ADU mandates. The city's suburban master-planned communities may have additional design review standards for accessory units.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Rancho Cucamonga as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

ADU Regulations in Elk Grove, California

In Sacramento County, Elk Grove permits ADUs by right under California's AB 68 and AB 3182. The city's planned neighborhoods have specific design guidelines that apply to new accessory units.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Elk Grove as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Elk Grove

Can I build an ADU in Elk Grove?

This California city follows the statewide AB 68 framework. Most single-family lots qualify for by-right ADU development.

How big can my ADU be in Elk Grove?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Elk Grove is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Elk Grove?

Plan review in Elk Grove typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

ADU Regulations in Arlington, Texas

Midway between Dallas and Fort Worth in Tarrant County, Arlington evaluates ADU applications under its local zoning ordinance. Property owners should verify district-specific rules since Texas lacks statewide ADU legislation.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Arlington

Can I build an ADU in Arlington?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

How big can my ADU be in Arlington?

Arlington allows detached ADUs up to 1,000 square feet on qualifying lots. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Arlington?

Plan review in Arlington typically runs 6 to 12 weeks, depending on how complete the application is. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Backlog in Tarrant County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

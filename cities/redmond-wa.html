ADU Regulations in Redmond, Washington

Home to Microsoft's headquarters in King County, Redmond permits ADUs under Washington's HB 1337. The city's Link Light Rail extension has created transit zones where parking requirements are waived for ADU projects.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Redmond as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

Regulation Requirement

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Redmond

Can I build an ADU in Redmond?

Redmond permits ADUs under Washington's HB 1337. Parking is not required near the Redmond Technology transit station.

How big can my ADU be in Redmond?

Redmond allows detached ADUs up to 1,000 square feet on qualifying lots. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Redmond?

Most ADU applications in Redmond clear plan review in 6 to 10 weeks. Simple attached conversions usually move faster than new detached construction. King County staff workload can push approvals toward the upper end of that range during busy months.

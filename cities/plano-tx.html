Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Plano

Can I build an ADU in Plano?

This Texas city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Verify your specific zoning district with the planning department before applying.

How big can my ADU be in Plano?

In Plano, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Plano?

Most ADU applications in Plano clear plan review in 6 to 12 weeks. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Backlog in Collin County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

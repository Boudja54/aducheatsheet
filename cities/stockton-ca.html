ADU Regulations in Stockton, California

Sitting in San Joaquin County, Stockton permits ADUs under California's Housing Accountability Act. The city's Development Services Department processes applications with consideration for historic district overlay zones that may impose additional design standards.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Stockton as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Stockton

Can I build an ADU in Stockton?

Stockton permits ADUs under California state law. Certain historic district properties may have additional design review requirements.

How big can my ADU be in Stockton?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Stockton is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Stockton?

Plan review in Stockton typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

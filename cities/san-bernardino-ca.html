ADU Regulations in San Bernardino, California

At the base of the San Bernardino Mountains, the city aligns with California's statewide ADU mandates. San Bernardino's Development Services Department processes applications under streamlined state timelines.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in San Bernardino as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in San Bernardino

Can I build an ADU in San Bernardino?

San Bernardino permits ADUs under California state law with minimal local restrictions beyond state requirements.

How big can my ADU be in San Bernardino?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in San Bernardino is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in San Bernardino?

Plan review in San Bernardino typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

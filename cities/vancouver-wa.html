ADU Regulations in Vancouver, Washington

Across the Columbia River from Portland, Vancouver enforces ADU regulations under Washington state law with specific local adjustments. The city requires off-street parking and owner occupancy, with design standards that complement the region's Pacific Northwest aesthetic.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Vancouver as of 2026. Regulations are governed by Washington's HB 1337 (2023) which limits local ADU restrictions statewide.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Vancouver

Can I build an ADU in Vancouver?

Vancouver, WA permits ADUs in residential zones. Owner occupancy is required and parking must be provided on-site.

How big can my ADU be in Vancouver?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Vancouver is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Vancouver?

Plan review in Vancouver typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

ADU Regulations in Modesto, California

Located in Stanislaus County, Modesto's ADU regulations align with California's statewide mandates. The city encourages detached ADUs as a solution to local housing needs, with most residential properties qualifying for by-right development under state law.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Modesto as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Modesto

Can I build an ADU in Modesto?

Modesto allows ADUs under California state law. The city does not impose additional restrictions beyond state minimums.

How big can my ADU be in Modesto?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Modesto is 1,200 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Modesto?

Plan review in Modesto typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

ADU Regulations in Bellevue, Washington

In King County across Lake Washington from Seattle, Bellevue maintains stringent ADU design guidelines. Despite Washington's HB 1337, the city enforces a 5-year owner occupancy requirement and detailed design review for exterior modifications.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Bellevue

Can I build an ADU in Bellevue?

Bellevue allows ADUs under strict design guidelines. The city requires owner occupancy for the first 5 years after permit issuance.

How big can my ADU be in Bellevue?

The maximum size for a detached ADU in Bellevue is 1,000 square feet, subject to setback requirements and lot coverage limits. Junior ADUs (JADUs) are limited to 500 square feet where permitted.

How long does the permit process take in Bellevue?

Plan review in Bellevue typically takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on application completeness and current workload. Simple attached ADU conversions may be processed faster than new detached construction.

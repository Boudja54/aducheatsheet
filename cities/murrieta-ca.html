ADU Regulations in Murrieta, California

In southwest Riverside County, Murrieta permits ADUs under California state law. The city's growing suburban neighborhoods welcome accessory units as part of the regional housing strategy.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Murrieta as of 2026. Regulations are governed by California's AB 68 (2020), AB 881 (2021), and AB 3182 (2024) which together establish a statewide ADU framework.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Murrieta

Can I build an ADU in Murrieta?

This California city follows the statewide AB 68 framework. Most single-family lots qualify for by-right ADU development.

How big can my ADU be in Murrieta?

Murrieta allows detached ADUs up to 1,200 square feet on qualifying lots. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Murrieta?

Plan review in Murrieta typically runs 8 to 12 weeks, depending on how complete the application is. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Backlog in Riverside County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.

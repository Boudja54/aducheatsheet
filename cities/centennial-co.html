Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Centennial

Can I build an ADU in Centennial?

This Colorado city regulates ADUs through local ordinance. Confirm the minimum lot size and owner occupancy rules with the planning department.

How big can my ADU be in Centennial?

Centennial allows detached ADUs up to 1,000 square feet on qualifying lots. Where junior ADUs (JADUs) are permitted, they are capped at 500 square feet under state law.

How long does the permit process take in Centennial?

Expect plan review in Centennial to take 4 to 8 weeks for a complete application. Attached ADU conversions are often processed ahead of detached builds. Arapahoe County processing times vary seasonally, so the range above is a realistic guide.

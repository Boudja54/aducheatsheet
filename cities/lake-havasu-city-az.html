Updated September 29, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Lake Havasu City

Can I build an ADU in Lake Havasu City?

This Arizona city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Confirm your zone's eligibility with the planning department first.

What is the maximum ADU size allowed in Lake Havasu City?

ADUs in Lake Havasu City can be up to 1,000 square feet, subject to the property's zoning and lot coverage limits. Always confirm the exact allowed size with the Lake Havasu City planning department before designing your project.

What are the setback requirements for an ADU in Lake Havasu City?

ADUs in Lake Havasu City generally require setbacks of 5 ft rear, 5 ft side. Arizona has no statewide ADU mandate — the exact setback figures come from Lake Havasu City's own zoning code, so confirm them with the city planning department before you design.

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Lake Havasu City

Can I build an ADU in Lake Havasu City?

This Arizona city permits ADUs under local ordinance. Confirm your zone's eligibility with the planning department first.

How big can my ADU be in Lake Havasu City?

In Lake Havasu City, the maximum footprint for a detached ADU is 1,000 square feet. When the property qualifies, a JADU conversion is capped at 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Lake Havasu City?

Expect plan review in Lake Havasu City to take 4 to 8 weeks for a complete application. Simple attached conversions usually move faster than new detached construction. Mohave County staff workload can push approvals toward the upper end of that range during busy months.

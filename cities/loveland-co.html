ADU Regulations in Loveland, Colorado

In Larimer County between Fort Collins and Greeley, Loveland permits ADUs through local zoning rules. With no Colorado statewide ADU law, the city's ordinance determines eligibility and standards.

Below is a summary of the key zoning rules applicable to Accessory Dwelling Units in Loveland as of 2026. Regulations are governed by local zoning ordinances, as Colorado has no statewide ADU mandate (HB 24-1152 addresses transit density).

Frequently Asked Questions About ADUs in Loveland

Can I build an ADU in Loveland?

This Colorado city regulates ADUs through local ordinance. Confirm the minimum lot size and owner occupancy rules with the planning department.

How big can my ADU be in Loveland?

Under Loveland zoning rules, a detached ADU can reach 1,000 square feet. When the property qualifies, a JADU conversion is capped at 500 square feet.

How long does the permit process take in Loveland?

Most ADU applications in Loveland clear plan review in 4 to 8 weeks. Simple attached conversions usually move faster than new detached construction. Backlog in Larimer County occasionally extends review times near the top of that window.
